This folder holds original, locally generated test media for the Phase 3 player.

- sample.mp4 is a solid-color H.264 clip with silent audio and muxed sample captions.
- poster.png is a matching still.
- captions.vtt is the sidecar WebVTT used on web.

None of these files are Guru Daiva Dutta recordings, stock footage, or third-party downloads.
Do not replace them with scraped or unauthorized videos.
