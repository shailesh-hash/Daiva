# 10 — Decisions and Open Questions

## Current assumptions

- Product name “Daiva Stories” is provisional.
- Initial content theme: Guru Daiva Dutta; exact cultural/language scope and source tradition should be confirmed with knowledgeable reviewers.
- Initial languages likely Kannada-first, with English metadata and later Telugu/Tamil support.
- Initial library: 5–10 episodes, ideally 1–5 minutes each; choose duration based on script and audience rather than forcing it.
- First cloud: AWS.
- First monetization: none or free pilot; defer subscription/coins until usage and rights are validated.
- Admin publishing is private and role-gated.
- AI assists discovery and drafts; humans retain editorial control.

## Product decisions to confirm before public launch

1. **Naming and trademark:** confirm app name, domain, store availability, and trademark risk.
2. **Cultural review:** identify a reviewer familiar with the relevant Guru Daiva Dutta tradition and regional context.
3. **Language:** Kannada-only at launch, or Kannada audio plus English/Telugu subtitles?
4. **Content production:** AI-generated visuals/narration, commissioned production, or licensed existing content?
5. **Content rights:** who owns script, visual, voice, music, translations, and generated assets? What licenses apply to AI provider outputs?
6. **Audience:** adults only, family/general audience, or children? If children may use it, obtain specialist privacy and age-appropriate design review.
7. **Identity:** allow anonymous browsing and require sign-in only for favorites/progress, or require sign-in early?
8. **Media provider:** AWS MediaConvert versus a managed video platform; compare pricing, regional availability, player support, and workload.
9. **Hosting region and privacy:** select based on user geography, applicable law, latency, and service availability.
10. **Monetization:** ads, subscription, episodic unlock, donations, or free? This changes entitlements, store policy, and compliance.
11. **Analytics:** minimum event set, retention, consent, and deletion rules.
12. **AI provider and budget:** select provider after testing Kannada quality, cost, latency, content safety, and data handling.

## Recommended launch order

1. Rights-cleared pilot content.
2. Browse and watch on web/mobile.
3. Measure playback quality and retention.
4. Add favorites/resume and basic sign-in.
5. Add deterministic search and AI-assisted discovery.
6. Add subscription only after validating demand and store/payment requirements.
